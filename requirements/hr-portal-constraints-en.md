# TCM Employee Portal — Demo Scope

## In scope (sample demo)
- Login + multilingual (Korean / English / Vietnamese) selection & switching
- Leave request & approval
- Announcements (view & post)

## Out of scope (excluded for this sample)
- Payslips, attendance lookup
- Real integration with attendance devices / payroll systems
- Admin dashboard, employee/org management

## Note
The real project covers the excluded items in Phase 1/2. Multilingual is demoed at the UI-text level.
